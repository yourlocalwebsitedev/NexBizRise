# Design review: nexbizrise.com (30 Sep 2026)

## Why it feels bland
1. **It looks like a template.** Every section follows the same pattern: a small teal eyebrow, a two-tone headline, and a beige background. Nothing is specific to NexBizRise or to local trades.
2. **There's no proof.** There are no real cards, client faces, logos, reviews or before/after numbers. The only stats ("4 sec · 100% · 24/7") are generic and sit in small grey tiles.
3. **There are no people and no places.** The page is text and icons only, with no plumber, salon, van or storefront. Your buyers are local business owners, and they want to see themselves on the page.
4. **The services section is a list of identical rows.** Five same-size cards with the same icon style and a "Learn more" link look like a settings page.
5. **The colour is low-energy.** The site is beige with a single teal. The ads use bold yellow (#FFC933), large black type and energy, but the site doesn't. Someone who clicks through from an ad lands on a different-looking brand.
6. **The headline says nothing specific.** "Helping Businesses Get More Customers" could be any agency's.
7. **The best product is hidden.** The digital card (the thing people can buy today for $49) is fifth in a list. There's no price and no card gallery on the home page.
8. **The typography is flat.** One sans font at similar weights everywhere, with no display moment and no contrast in scale.

## What to change (in priority order)
1. **A specific, trade-level hero.** For example, "Every missed call becomes a booked job." Show one real card and a phone receiving the auto-text side by side, with two CTAs: "Book a free demo" and "Get my card — $49".
2. **Adopt the ad palette site-wide.** Keep ink and cream as the base, make yellow the action colour, and use teal only for small accents. The site and ads will then look like one brand.
3. **Add a "Built for your trade" strip.** Tiles for plumber, realtor, salon, clinic, lawyer and founder, each opening that trade's card design. (The designs already exist in Card Designs by Business.)
4. **Add a live card gallery on the home page.** A scrolling row of 6–8 real cards in phone frames. It's the most convincing thing you have.
5. **Replace the stats with a money story.** For example: "5 missed calls a week × $285 = $1,425 lost." It's the same maths as the ads and it lands harder than "100%".
6. **Show pricing openly.** Digital $49, Motion $79, and "Missed-call text-back: book a demo". Being transparent builds trust with small-business owners.
7. **Add proof.** 3 short testimonials with a face, name, trade and city; before you have clients, show "Cards made this month" or founder-verified examples. Add trust marks for Stripe and Razorpay, and "Cancel anytime".
8. **Vary the layout rhythm.** Alternate dark ink sections, yellow feature bands and cream. Use full-width imagery, a bento grid for services instead of a list, and one big display headline per section.
9. **Use a display font for headlines.** Pair a characterful display face for headlines with the current sans for body text.
10. **Add small moments of life.** The coin-drip or text-back animation from the ads could appear as a looping micro-scene in the hero or the missed-call section.

## Quick wins (under a day)
- Change the headline and add the "$49 card" CTA to the hero.
- Make the primary buttons yellow.
- Move the Digital Card up to first or second in services.
- Add the card gallery row, which already exists on the tap-card page, to the home page.
- Add real photos of trades from Pexels or Unsplash. I can't generate photos.
